About St. Lawrence Junior School Lira

Nurturing future leaders through academic excellence, discipline, and digital learning in Northern Uganda.

🌟 Vision & Mission

Vision: To be a leading center of academic excellence and holistic child development in Uganda.

Mission: To provide quality, affordable, and practical education that equips every learner with moral integrity and vital skills.

💡 Philosophy & Core Values

Philosophy: Every child is unique, capable, and deserves holistic quality education.

Core Values:

  • God-fearing & Moral Integrity
  • Academic Excellence & Hard Work
  • Discipline & Respect
  • Digital Literacy & Innovation

🏫 Campus & Facilities

Our campus is equipped with modern learning infrastructure designed to support comprehensive pupil growth:

  • Modern Computer & ICT Laboratory
  • Well-stocked Primary & Nursery Sections
  • Spacious Sports Grounds for Athletics & Football
  • Secure Boarding & Day Facilities
